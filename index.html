Questions Students Ask

Frequently Asked Questions

Can students with an Alim qualification apply to universities in Malaysia?

Potentially, depending on the university, programme, awarding body, subject results and qualification evaluation. Admission is determined by the institution.

Can Qawmi madrasha students study abroad?

Individual review is important. Acceptance depends on the specific credential, recognition, supporting qualifications and institutional policy. Contact us with your documents for a more accurate assessment.

Is IELTS always mandatory?

English requirements vary by institution and programme. Some institutions may recognise alternative tests or offer conditional pathways, but any exemption must be confirmed officially.

Are scholarships guaranteed?

No. Scholarships are competitive and depend on availability, eligibility and university decisions. We can help you identify opportunities to investigate.

Do you assist parents and guardians?

Yes. We can discuss study pathways, relevant documents, expected costs and next steps with families.
